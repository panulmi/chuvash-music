# Thumbnail guide

## Style direction
- warm lights and deep blue backgrounds
- strong central subject or portrait
- cinematic indie aesthetic
- subtle grain and soft blur
- text limited to 2–3 words

## Suggested text styles
- large serif or bold sans serif
- high contrast color choices
- place title at the center or lower third

## Example titles
- Cerlehi Chun
- Chuvash Nights
- Yulashki Cul
- Kucsenche Hevel

## Good color combinations
- gold + navy
- burnt orange + deep blue
- cream + dark teal
- warm red + charcoal
